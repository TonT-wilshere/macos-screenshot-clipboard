SnapKeep · 截屏即贴

中文
将 SnapKeep.app 拖入“应用程序”，打开后点击“启用 / 更新截屏即贴”。
首次使用请允许 SnapKeep 控制 System Events（系统事件）。
照常用 ⌘⇧3 / ⌘⇧4 / ⌘⇧5 截屏：文件保存到桌面“截屏”，图片自动复制到剪贴板。
可以退出 SnapKeep，自动复制继续生效。卸载请重新打开应用并点击“卸载并恢复设置”。
本独立开源构建未经 Apple 公证。首次打开若被拦截，按 Apple 指引在“系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 仍要打开”放行。

English
Drag SnapKeep.app to Applications. Open it and click “Enable / Update SnapKeep”.
Allow SnapKeep to control System Events when asked.
Use ⌘⇧3 / ⌘⇧4 / ⌘⇧5 as usual. Screenshots are saved to Desktop/截屏 and copied to the clipboard.
You can quit the app after setup. Reopen it to uninstall and restore the previous save settings.
This independent open-source build is not Apple-notarized. If blocked, follow Apple's “Open Anyway” instructions in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
